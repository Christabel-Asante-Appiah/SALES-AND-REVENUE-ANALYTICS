Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard

The Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of product sales performance, customer demographics, and revenue trends. It enables business users to monitor key sales metrics, analyze product category performance, and identify customer purchasing patterns to support data-driven decision-making.

Key features include:

Sales Performance Overview: Displays total quantity sold, total revenue generated, total customers served, and average customer age.
Product Category Analysis: Compares revenue and quantity sold across product categories such as Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty, helping identify top-performing products.
Product Composition Insights: Visualizes the contribution of each product category to overall sales volume, enabling users to understand product mix and demand distribution.
Sales Trend Monitoring: Tracks monthly sales quantity and revenue trends, allowing users to identify peak and low-performing periods throughout the year.
Customer Segmentation: Includes filters for Gender and Generation, enabling deeper analysis of purchasing behavior across different customer groups.
Automated Business Insights: Highlights key findings, such as top-performing product categories, highest and lowest revenue periods, and average sales performance.

The dashboard serves as a powerful analytical tool for sales teams, retail managers, and business stakeholders seeking to optimize product strategies, improve customer targeting, and maximize revenue growth.
